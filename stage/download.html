Open Ava Stage the first time

Read this before you open Ava Stage. You do this once for each new version.

Check that this file is for your Mac

Apple menu → About This Mac.

“Chip: Apple M…” → use Ava Stage for Apple silicon.

“Processor: Intel” → use Ava Stage for Intel.

Ava Stage does not work on macOS older than 13. About This Mac shows your version.

Drag Ava Stage to Applications

In the window of this download, drag Ava Stage onto Applications.

Open Ava Stage from Applications

Stop if macOS says Ava Stage is damaged or harmful. Do not open it. Keep using your previous Ava and write to your Ava contact.

Apple has not checked Ava Stage, so macOS may block it the first time. If you see this box, click Done, not Move to Trash. If Ava Stage opens, go to step 5.

Open it once in System Settings

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down. Next to “Ava Stage” was blocked, click Open Anyway. If macOS asks, use Touch ID or type your Mac password. The warning appears again: click Open. If Open Anyway is not there, write to your Ava contact.

Ava Stage opens on Setup the first time

Next time, open Ava Stage from Applications.

Good to know

You do not need Terminal to open Ava Stage. Do not switch off a protection of your Mac to open it.

Stuck? Write to your Ava contact.
